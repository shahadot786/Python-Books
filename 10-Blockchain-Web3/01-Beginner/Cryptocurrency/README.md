# 💰 Cryptocurrency

Understanding digital currencies and their ecosystems.

## 🗺️ Learning Roadmap

### 1. Bitcoin Fundamentals
- Bitcoin whitepaper and history
- UTXO model
- Mining and difficulty adjustment

### 2. Ethereum Basics
- Account-based model vs UTXO
- Ether and gas
- Smart contracts introduction

### 3. Wallets & Security
- Hot wallets vs cold wallets
- Hardware wallets (Ledger, Trezor)
- Seed phrases and key management

### 4. Exchanges & Stablecoins
- Centralized exchanges (CEX)
- Decentralized exchanges (DEX)
- Stablecoins (USDC, DAI, USDT)
